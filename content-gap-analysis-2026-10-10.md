## Gaps per cluster

### Cluster A gaps — AI chatbots
**What we cover:** 21 service niches with text-chat chatbot angles.
**What we're missing:**
1. **AI voice receptionist / AI phone agents** — the hottest 2026 trend. 800.com launched a free AI-agent trial Oct 7, 2026; Voiply launched an AI Receptionist add-on; Goldman Sachs 2026 survey cited widely. We have ZERO voice/phone-agent content. This is the single biggest topical gap.
2. **Underserved high-ticket niches:** med-spa/aesthetic clinics, chiropractors, optometrists, mental-health therapists, senior home-care agencies, childcare/daycare centres.
3. **Trades we skipped:** HVAC technicians, electricians, auto-repair shops, pest-control, landscaping companies, moving companies, mortgage brokers, insurance agents, accounting firms.
4. **Comparison content:** "best AI chatbot for small business" — dominated by vendor blogs (hyperleap.ai, dante-ai.com, albato.com, designloud.com). We have no best-of/comparison article, which is a classic buyer-intent magnet.

### Cluster B gaps — Instagram DM automation
**What we cover:** 6 beauty/creative niches.
**What we're missing:**
1. **Fitness coaches / personal trainers** — leadresponse.co ranks for gym Instagram DM content; huge buyer-intent niche, uncovered by us.
2. **E-commerce comment-to-DM** — "comment LINK to get the link" automation is exploding (AP3K, StarLovin launches in 2026); medium.com roundup of tools exists but no small-business how-to from a neutral educator.
3. **Real-estate agents on Instagram**, **restaurants/cafés**, **coaches/creators selling lead magnets** — all named as core comment-to-DM users (linkedin.com practitioner post, Oct 2026).

### Cluster C gaps — WhatsApp automation
**What we cover:** 12 retail/service niches, all "automation for X" angle.
**What we're missing:**
1. **WhatsApp Business API setup guide** — ChatDaddy, BizAI, aapta.in all rank detailed 2026 setup guides (Meta verification, BSP choice, templates). We explain the *benefit* per niche but never the *how-to*. High search demand, education-first fit.
2. **WhatsApp broadcast marketing playbook** — AiSensy, setsmart.io, storefries.com rank full guides (list building, templates, ban-avoidance). We mention broadcasts in passing; no dedicated guide.
3. **Niche gaps on WhatsApp:** salons/spas, dental/clinics, gyms, schools, real-estate — all high-volume WhatsApp markets we haven't touched on this channel.
4. **Click-to-WhatsApp ads** — mentioned by vendors as list-growth engine; no educator content from us.

### Cluster D gaps — General automation
**What we cover:** 1 ecommerce guide, 1 lead-capture guide, 1 general n8n guide.
**What we're missing:**
1. **n8n + WhatsApp specific tutorial** — dev.to has multiple 2026 guides ("30-minute AI WhatsApp bot with n8n", "official Cloud API + n8n"). Our n8n article is general; the specific high-demand integration tutorial is missing.
2. **Missed-call text-back automation** — vendors cite "$12.15 per missed call / $26k+ annual losses" (Ambs Call Center via Voiply). No dedicated article on the missed-call recovery automation.
3. **ManyChat alternatives / tool comparisons** — setsmart.io ranks "ManyChat alternative for agencies". Comparison content builds topical authority.
