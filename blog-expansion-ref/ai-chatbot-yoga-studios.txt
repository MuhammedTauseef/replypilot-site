The Injury and Pregnancy Questions: Handle With Care

Two categories of questions need special design: injuries and pregnancy. These arrive constantly, they are sensitive, and the wrong answer is worse than no answer. A good yoga studio chatbot treats them with a fixed, careful pattern. For injuries: acknowledge, ask which class they are considering, explain that teachers offer modifications for common issues, share which classes are gentlest, and always add that they should check with their doctor or physio first and tell the teacher before class. For pregnancy: point to the dedicated prenatal classes if you have them, explain which regular classes are suitable in which trimester according to your teachers' guidance, and again, doctor first.

What the bot must never do is play doctor. \

What If Someone Has a Serious Injury?

Then the bot does the safest and most helpful thing: it shares your studio's written guidance about which classes are gentlest, tells them to check with their doctor or physio first, asks them to tell the teacher before class, and offers to connect them with a human to discuss further.
