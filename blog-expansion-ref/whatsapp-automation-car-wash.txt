Frequently Asked Questions

How does a car wash WhatsApp booking chatbot actually work?

You add your packages and free time slots once. When a customer messages, the chatbot shows the packages and open slots, takes the booking, and sends a confirmation with a reference number. You just watch the schedule fill up while you wash.

Can WhatsApp automation tell customers when their car is ready?

Yes, and customers love this feature most. Your staff taps one button when the wash starts and finishes, and the customer gets an instant message. They stop crowding your counter asking whether their car is done.

What if it rains for a week and my car wash goes quiet?

The system keeps your customer list, so you can send a well-timed offer to exactly the right people — past customers who have not visited in a while. One message after a dust storm or rainy spell can fill a whole day of empty bays.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API for car wash automation?

For real automation with bookings and broadcasts, yes — the regular WhatsApp Business app is too limited. Your provider handles the API approval and setup for you. It usually takes a few days and needs a Facebook Business account.

Can the chatbot sell monthly wash packages for my car wash?

Yes. It explains the package, takes the signup, tracks how many washes the customer has used, and reminds them before the month ends. Weekly plans for drivers and fleet plans for companies work the same way.
