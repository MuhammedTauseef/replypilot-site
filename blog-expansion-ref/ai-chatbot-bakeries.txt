How Is AI Being Used in Bakeries?

When people ask how AI is being used in bakeries, the answer is simpler than they expect. It is not robots baking bread. In real bakeries, AI mostly means a chatbot on the website or WhatsApp that takes custom cake orders, answers questions about ingredients and allergens, quotes prices for standard sizes, and sends pickup reminders. The oven still belongs to you. The AI just makes sure no order dies in an unread message while you are elbow-deep in dough.
